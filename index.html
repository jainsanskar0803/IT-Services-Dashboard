Strategic growth drivers
FY2025–FY2027
Forecast
Cloud Migration: Enterprise cloud adoption globally remains ~35%. All five companies are positioned for multi-year migration contracts — a $50B+ cumulative opportunity through FY2027.
Gen AI Monetisation: Accenture disclosed $3B+ in AI bookings in FY24. TCS AI.Cloud and Infosys Topaz are ramping. Gen AI could add 2–4% incremental revenue CAGR by FY27 for sector leaders.
BFSI Recovery: North American BFSI — 20–30% of revenues for all five firms — is projected to recover from H2 FY25, disproportionately benefiting HCL Tech and Wipro given their BFSI concentration.
Margin Tailwind: Post-FY24 headcount rationalisation improves revenue per employee 4–6% sector-wide — compounding into structural margin improvement through FY26–27.
